# Style Catalog Reference

Style labels are shortcuts, not art direction. Use them only with camera/light/composition constraints. Never as a replacement.

## Photographic Realism
- Photorealistic — documentary, neutral
- Cinematic — directed light, shallow DOF, narrative
- Monochrome Noir — high contrast B&W, dramatic
- Golden Motion — warm directional, motion blur feel

## Dimensional / 3D
- Geometric 3D — hard surfaces, clean renders
- Whimsical 3D — soft, rounded, playful
- Diorama — miniature sets, shallow DOF, overhead angle
- Isometric 3D — technical, no perspective convergence

## Illustrated Narrative
- Anime — line-driven, flat color, expressive
- Retroprint — limited palette, halftone, vintage print
- Flat Design — geometric, no shading, bold color
- Impressionism — visible brushwork, light-focused

## Graphic / Simplified
- Icon Play — minimal, symbolic, reduced forms
- Pattern Dot — pointillist, repetitive structure
- Chroma Key — high saturation, flat, graphic
- Toy Form — simplified volumes, toy-like proportions

## Drawn / Drafted
- Technical Sketch — line drawing, annotations, blueprint feel
- Doodle — loose, hand-drawn, informal
- Line Drawing — clean contour, minimal shading
- Accent Line — line + single color accent

Remember: style names mean nothing without light, camera, and composition decisions.
